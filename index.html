KhataGST

GST filing and ITC reconciliation for Indian MSMEs

GitHub
  • Gemini Vision turns photos of invoices into structured records (GSTIN, tax split, vendor details), validated on both client and server.
  • GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B are computed in plain deterministic code using integer paise instead of floats, with Excel/CSV export.
  • AI CA assistant grounded in GST law (CGST Act Sections 16, 17(5) and 50; Rules 88A and 86B) that returns the exact sections behind every answer.
  • Confidence-routed extraction with a fallback chain across Gemini models, so a failed scan becomes a review form, never an error.
